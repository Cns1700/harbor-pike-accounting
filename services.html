What we handle

Services

We offer two services and will talk through the year first with you to assess the situation.
(This page is only a layout of services & description, not a price list.)

Service groups

Personal tax

Annual returns for wages, side work, rental property, and the usual Gulf Coast mix of 1099s. We prepare the filing, walk through what changed from last year, and keep a copy on file so next spring is not a scavenger hunt.

Tax planning

A short planning meeting before year-end, not a lecture in April. We look at withholding, estimated payments, and the choices that still have time to matter — retirement contributions, equipment, and how a new job or a sale will land on the return.

Bookkeeping

Monthly closes for shops, crews, and offices that need clean categories more than a fancy dashboard. Bank recs, bills, and a report you can read. The point is a set of books we can both trust when tax season arrives.

Payroll

Regular pay runs, deposits, and the filings that go with them. We keep the calendar, so payday does not depend on someone remembering a due date. New hires and the occasional bonus are part of the same work.

Quarterly estimates

Estimated tax drawn from the books you already have, with dates on a calendar. We would rather send four boring payments than one painful catch-up. If the year turns, we adjust the next voucher instead of guessing.
